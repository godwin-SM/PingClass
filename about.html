About PingClass

Focused software for tuition and coaching institutes.

PingClass is a simple dashboard for running a tuition or coaching institute. It brings students, batches, attendance, fees, teachers and parents into one place — instead of a stack of registers, spreadsheets and scattered messages.

Who it's for

Small and mid-sized tuition and coaching institutes that want clarity without complexity. Owners, administrators and teachers who spend too much time on attendance sheets and fee reminders and not enough on teaching.

What makes it different

  • One focused dashboard. Students, batches, attendance and fees — with admin, teacher and parent views built in. No modules you will never open.
  • Built for the Indian tuition owner. Fees in rupees, batch-based attendance, parent communication that fits how institutes actually run.
  • Free to start. Free plan for up to 20 students. Paid plans at ₹249 and ₹599 per month when you grow.

The problem we're solving

Most institutes run on paper registers, copied spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads. Attendance gets missed, fee reminders chase everyone, and the owner ends up being the only person who knows what's actually happening. PingClass exists so an institute can be run calmly, with accurate attendance and fees, without hiring extra people or learning heavy software.

How PingClass works

  • Add students and organise them into batches.
  • Take batch-wise attendance and instantly see who is falling behind.
  • Track fees — collected, pending and overdue — and send reminders automatically.
  • Give teachers and parents their own dashboards so the admin is not the messenger for everything.

Pricing

  • Free — up to 20 students, ₹0.
  • Basic — ₹249/month for growing institutes.
  • Pro — ₹599/month, everything included.

No contracts, cancel anytime.
