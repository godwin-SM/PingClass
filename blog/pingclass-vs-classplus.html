PingClass vs Classplus: Which Tuition Management Software Fits Your Size?

Software · 6 min read · Updated September 2026

Classplus is the biggest name in Indian tuition management — a heavily funded platform that powers thousands of coaching institutes. PingClass is a small, self-funded alternative built around one idea: most institutes do not need most features. Which one fits depends on your size, your budget and how much complexity you actually want to run.

What Classplus does well

  • Branded app. Your institute gets its own branded app, which looks professional to parents and students.
  • Built-in payment gateway. Collect fees inside the platform — no separate payment setup or reconciliation.
  • Large user base. A big community, established brand and proven track record with thousands of institutes.
  • Robust course and fee features. Batch management, course catalogs, fee structures and reports built for scale.
  • India-focused. Pricing, payment methods, SMS and parent communication are all tuned to the Indian market.

Where Classplus costs more

Classplus is a serious product, and it is priced that way:

  • Pricing starts higher. The free or low tiers are limited; the plans that unlock real features cost more from day one.
  • Long-term contracts. You commit for a period, not month to month. That is a real risk for a small institute trying a tool for the first time.
  • Per-seat onboarding. Setup and onboarding are charged per seat, so the price climbs as your institute grows.
  • Features behind higher tiers. What you actually want is often gated behind a more expensive plan than you started with.
  • Complex for a two-teacher institute. Courses, catalogs, catalogs of catalogs — the depth becomes overhead when you just want attendance and fees.

None of that makes Classplus bad. It makes it a tool sized for a specific kind of institute — and small, simple centers pay for power they never use.

What PingClass does well

  • Free for up to 20 students. The free tier is a working product, not a trial — no time limit, no card required.
  • No contract. Month to month, simple paid plans, and you can walk away any time.
  • Attendance + fees + parent app in one place. The three things a small institute actually runs on, under one roof.
  • Simple setup. Sign up, add your batches, add students. You are running within the hour, with no onboarding calls.
  • Works globally. No Indian tax complexity baked in — the same dashboard works for an institute anywhere.

Where PingClass falls short

  • No branded mobile app for institutes. Parents use the parent app, but your institute does not get its own white-label app yet.
  • No payment gateway integration yet. Fees are tracked in the dashboard; collection still happens outside the tool.
  • Smaller community. Fewer institutes, fewer tutorials, less support content than a platform with Classplus's scale.
  • No enterprise SSO. If you need SAML and centralized login for a large chain, that capability is not there.

The honest recommendation

Use Classplus if you are running 50+ students, want a branded app parents open on their phones, and want a payment gateway built into the product. The cost and the complexity are justified when the platform is doing real work at real scale.

Use PingClass if you are under 50 students, want to start free, and prefer simplicity over feature density. A two-teacher institute does not need a course catalog — it needs to know who came today and who has not paid.

Both tools are fine choices. Size is the real deciding factor — everything else is the platform that matches the size.

The bottom line

Classplus and PingClass solve the same problem at different scales. If you are growing toward 50+ students, Classplus earns its price. If you are small and want to stay simple, PingClass gets you the same core record — attendance, fees, parents — free, with no contract to escape.

Both tools are fine choices. PingClass is free for up to 20 students — see if the simplicity fits.
